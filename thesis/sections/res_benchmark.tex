\section{Two defects in the benchmark}\label{sec:res:benchmark}
% A service contribution: both affect anyone else's SafeLIBERO level-II numbers.
%
%  1. NON-DETERMINISM. SafeLIBERO parks unused obstacles off-scene in a heap. Left collidable they
%     overflow MuJoCo's contact buffer (ncon = 5000), and on overflow MuJoCo drops contacts
%     order-dependently, so identical episodes scored differently in 6 of 26 re-runs (23\%).
%     Fixed by clearing contype/conaffinity on parked bodies.
%  2. PHANTOM COLLISIONS. Objects spawning unsupported fall under gravity with the arm stationary
%     --- a moka pot fell 102\,mm --- and the displacement is attributed to the robot. Fixed with
%     60 settling steps before measurement begins; residual 0.00\,mm.
%     Ordering matters: the settle must run AFTER parked contacts are disabled.
